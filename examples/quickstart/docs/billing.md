# Billing, credits, and platform fees

Billing is credit-based. The signed-in dashboard's [Billing page](https://app.nrouter.ai) shows your balance, plans, payment methods, auto top-up, and invoices. Current prices can change; use the [nRouter pricing page](https://nrouter.ai/pricing) for current plan details.

## Pay as you go

- Add credits starting at **$5**. There is no subscription commitment.
- Pay as you go currently has a **4% platform fee**, added on top of the credits purchased; the minimum top-up is $5.
- Pro currently has a **0% platform fee**. Enterprise terms are custom.
- Requests use credits at the model provider's listed price; nRouter does not add a per-token model markup.
- Owners and admins can view Billing. Only owners can buy credits, change plans, and manage payment methods; owners and admins can manage auto top-up.
- Manage Subscription and Manage Payment Methods open the Stripe Customer Portal. Invoices can be viewed and downloaded from Billing.

## Enterprise

Enterprise pricing and terms are custom. Check the pricing page or contact sales for a quote.

## Current pricing

Fees and plan terms can change. For the latest details, use the [nRouter pricing page](https://nrouter.ai/pricing).
